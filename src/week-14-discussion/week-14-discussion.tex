\LARGE Interpreting \& Organizing Results
\normalsize ~

We have reached the part of the semester where you should be writing up the results of your project.  To help get you there, let's try a few exercises.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Let's take 10-15 minutes to list 3-5 key results from your project.  This can be supported by figures, stats, or general observations.  For each, write the direct claim that the result supports, providing the evidence (numbers, figures, etc), and any limitations or uncertainties.

\vspace{8in}

\item Now that you have these in hand, pair up and review each other's results.  Each partner needs to pick one surprising or ambiguous result and list possible explanations, confounding variables, and suggest additional analyses that would clarify the result or interpretation.

\vspace{5in}

\item Finally, write a one-sentence headline claim of your own results, and one key uncertainty that still needs to be addressed.  Propose these to each other for feedback.  Be prepared to share your headline with the class before we break for the day.

\end{enumerate}
